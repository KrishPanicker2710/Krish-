About Greek Mythology

Greek mythology is a collection of myths and legends from ancient Greece. These stories explain the origins of the world, natural events, and the adventures of gods, goddesses, heroes, and mythical creatures. The Greek gods were believed to live on Mount Olympus, with Zeus as their king. Other important gods included Poseidon, Athena, and Hades. Greek mythology also includes famous heroes such as Heracles, Perseus, and Odysseus. These myths have greatly influenced literature, art, and culture around the world.
